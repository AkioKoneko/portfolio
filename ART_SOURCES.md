# Art sources used in the local portfolio

Only official Warspear Online artwork is integrated into the page background.
Player-made art was reviewed as a possible direction but was not copied or used
because the forum rules do not grant permission for external reuse.

## Official cavern background

- Local file: `assets/images/ambient/official_cavern.jpg`
- Direct source: https://warspear-online.com/assets/img/pic_bg_index_desktop.jpg
- Page owner: official Warspear Online website
- Use: low-opacity framed background art on wide screens

## Official 2026 anniversary dragon artwork

- Local file: `assets/images/ambient/official_dragon_2026.png`
- Direct source: https://forum.warspear-online.com/uploads/monthly_2026_05/image.thumb.png.7f15e1aa2120cd8825b1826c73c57bca.png
- Announcement: https://forum.warspear-online.com/index.php?/topic/478109-20260529-warspear-online-update-134-song-of-the-spear-announcement/
- Page owner: Warspear Online administrators
- Use: low-opacity framed background art on wide screens

## Fan-art policy checked

- Rules: https://forum.warspear-online.com/index.php?/topic/92595-rules-of-the-section-fan-art/
- Decision: do not reuse player art without the artist's permission, even with a
  credit link.
